🎲

Roll, Move, and Claim Sri Lanka

Roll the 3D dice to travel counter-clockwise around 40 iconic Sri Lankan locations—from Galle Face Green and Kandy Lake to Sigiriya and Jaffna Fort.

  • Unowned land? Buy it instantly or pass it to a live player auction!
  • Avurudu Start: Collect +LKR 2,000 bonus salary every time you loop the island.
  • Doubles Bonus: Roll doubles for an immediate extra roll! 3 doubles sends you to CID inquiry.
🏦

Serendib Credit & Disaster Shields

Experience realistic Sri Lankan economic resilience with credit desks and insurance policies.

  • Low on cash? Visit the Bank of Serendib tab to borrow up to LKR 10,000 at 10-15% flat rate to keep buying properties.
  • Monsoon & Cyclones: Weather events can storm-damage buildings! Buy Disaster Shields in the Shield tab for 80-100% instant compensation.
  • Poya Holiday Pool: All island taxes pool into the Poya fund; landing on Poya pays the entire jackpot to you!
🏨

Monopolies & Luxury Boutique Hotels

Form complete color monopolies to unlock special district perks and multiply rents exponentially.

  • District Monopoly: Owning all 2-3 properties of a color doubles their base rent automatically!
  • Build Boutique Hotels: Add houses and upgrade to a 5-star Hotel to charge massive rents up to LKR 20,000+ per visit.
  • Win Condition: Bankrupt your opponents or amass the highest Net Worth before rounds run out!
